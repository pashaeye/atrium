Atrium

Vertical tabs, Spaces and a sidebar for Chrome.

Atrium turns Chrome’s side panel into a calm home for your tabs: Spaces for each part of your life, Favorites and Pinned tabs that stay put, a Command Bar for tabs and history, Peek for quick looks, a focus timer and screenshots.
